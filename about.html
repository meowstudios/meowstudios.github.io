ABOUT MEOW STUDIOS

A small family.
A lot of games.

Meow Studios is an independent, family-run game studio from Romania, creating colorful casual games for players around the world.

Independent by choice.

After more than a decade of working in game development, we started Meow Studios with a simple goal: to make fun, welcoming games that we can be proud of and players can enjoy for years to come. Staying small allows us to remain directly involved in the games we create while keeping the creative freedom that made us start Meow Studios in the first place.
