ABOUT MEOW STUDIOS

A small family.
A lot of games.

Meow Studios is an independent, family-run game studio from Romania. We create colorful casual games designed to be fun, relaxing and easy to enjoy wherever you are.

Behind the games is a very small team, a lot of coffee, plenty of ideas and probably more cats than strictly necessary.

OUR STORY

Independent by choice.

Meow Studios grew out of a long-standing love for making games. After years of working in game development, we wanted to build something of our own — a studio where we could choose the games we create, make decisions quickly and stay directly involved in every part of the process.

Staying small gives us that freedom. Instead of separating game development between large departments, we work closely across design, development, artwork, publishing and day-to-day operations.

It also means that when you play one of our games, you are playing something made by the same people who designed it, built it, tested it and continue improving it.
