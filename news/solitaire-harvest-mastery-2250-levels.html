Solitaire Harvest Mastery Reaches 2,250 Levels

Solitaire Harvest Mastery has reached 2,250 playable levels, marking another milestone as the game continues to grow with new farms, environments and TriPeaks solitaire challenges.

We’re happy to share that Solitaire Harvest Mastery has now grown to 2,250 levels.

Since its release, we’ve continued expanding the game with new levels, farms and environments while keeping the classic TriPeaks solitaire gameplay at the center of the experience.

Players can progress through 15 different farm types, featuring settings inspired by everything from traditional countryside farms to tropical and Asian-themed environments. Along the way, they can encounter farms built around cows, chickens, apples, strawberries and many other themes.

More to come

Reaching 2,250 levels is an important milestone for us, but development continues. We plan to keep adding new content and expanding the game over time.

Solitaire Harvest Mastery is available on mobile platforms and can be played both online and offline. App Store | Google Play
