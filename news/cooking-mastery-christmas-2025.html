Christmas Magic Arrives in Cooking Mastery

Christmas has arrived in Cooking Mastery with a special holiday restaurant, storybook-inspired recipes and a brand-new seasonal challenge.

The most magical time of the year has arrived in Cooking Mastery, and we've prepared something special for the holiday season.

This year's Christmas event introduces a festive restaurant filled with recipes inspired by the stories, flavors and memories that make Christmas feel like Christmas.

A restaurant full of Christmas magic

The new holiday restaurant takes players into a warm and colorful Christmas setting, with special recipes designed to bring back a little of the magic of childhood.

From the festive atmosphere to the food being prepared, the restaurant was created to feel like stepping into a Christmas story, while keeping the fast-paced cooking gameplay at the heart of Cooking Mastery.

A new Christmas challenge

Alongside the seasonal restaurant, players can take part in a new Christmas challenge created especially for the event.

It's our way of celebrating the holidays with the Cooking Mastery community and giving players something new to discover during the Christmas season.

Merry Christmas and happy cooking!
