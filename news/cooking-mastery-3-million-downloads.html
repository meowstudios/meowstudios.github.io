Cooking Mastery Surpasses 3 Million Downloads Worldwide

Cooking Mastery has surpassed 3 million downloads worldwide, marking a major milestone for our restaurant cooking game.

We’re happy to share that Cooking Mastery has now surpassed 3 million downloads worldwide.

Since its release, millions of players have stepped into Cooking Mastery’s restaurants to prepare dishes, serve customers and build their way through an expanding collection of restaurants and locations.

Over the years, we’ve continued to expand the game with new restaurants, recipes, ingredients and challenges while continuing to improve the experience for both new and returning players.

A milestone that means a lot to us

Reaching 3 million downloads is an important milestone for Cooking Mastery and for us as an independent studio. We’re grateful to everyone who has downloaded, played and supported the game over the years.

Development of Cooking Mastery continues, with more content and improvements planned for the future.

Cooking Mastery is available across mobile platforms. App Store | Google Play
