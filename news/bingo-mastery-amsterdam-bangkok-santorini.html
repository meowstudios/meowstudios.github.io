Bingo Mastery Adds Three New Locations

Bingo Mastery continues its journey around the world with three new destinations: Amsterdam, Bangkok and Santorini.

We’ve added three new locations to Bingo Mastery, giving players even more destinations to discover as they progress through the game.

The latest update takes players to Amsterdam, Bangkok and Santorini, with each destination featuring its own colorful setting inspired by the character and landmarks of the real-world location.

Three new destinations

Players can travel through the canals and colorful streets of Amsterdam, experience the vibrant atmosphere of Bangkok, and continue their journey among the whitewashed buildings and blue waters of Santorini.

These new locations join Bingo Mastery’s growing collection of destinations from around the world, as we continue to expand the game with new places and content.

More places to explore

Exploring new destinations has always been part of the experience in Bingo Mastery, and we plan to continue adding new locations for players to visit in future updates.
