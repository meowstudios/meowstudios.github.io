Meow Studios Joins the Romanian Game Developers Association

September 3, 2026

Meow Studios is now officially a member of the Romanian Game Developers Association (RGDA).

We’re happy to join a community that brings together game studios, developers, and professionals from across the Romanian games industry.

For us, becoming part of RGDA is another step in strengthening our connection with the local game development community as Meow Studios continues to grow and expand its portfolio of games.

Part of Romania’s Game Development Community

RGDA represents and supports the Romanian game development ecosystem, bringing together companies and professionals working across the industry.

We look forward to being part of the community, meeting fellow developers and studios, sharing experiences, and contributing along the way.

Thank you to RGDA for welcoming Meow Studios!

About Meow Studios

Meow Studios is an independent mobile game development studio based in Romania. The studio creates and operates casual games for players around the world, including Cooking Mastery, Solitaire Harvest Mastery, and Bingo Mastery.

Rather than focusing solely on one-time releases, Meow Studios follows a long-term approach to game development, continuously expanding its games with new content, features, seasonal events, and gameplay improvements.

Learn more at www.meowstudios.org.
